About DriveHub

DriveHub is a multi vendor vehicle marketplace built for the Bangladeshi car market.
Verified vendors list their vehicles and customers browse, compare, book a test drive
and buy with confidence. Every listing is moderated by our employees before it goes live.

How it works

Three simple steps.

Step 1: Register

Open Register, choose Customer, Vendor or Employee, and fill in the details
that your type needs. A vendor also uploads a trade licence.

Step 2: Log in

Use the single login page. DriveHub opens your own dashboard automatically.
Vendors and employees log in after an admin approves them.

Step 3: Use the marketplace

Browse and compare cars, list a car for sale, moderate listings or manage
the whole platform, depending on your account.

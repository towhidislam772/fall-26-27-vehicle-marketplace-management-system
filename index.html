How it works

Three simple steps.

1

Register

Open Register, choose Customer, Vendor or Employee, and fill in the details that your type needs. A vendor also uploads a trade licence.

2

Log in

Use the single login page. DriveHub opens your own dashboard automatically. Vendors and employees log in after an admin approves them.

3

Use the marketplace

Browse and compare cars, list a car for sale, moderate listings or manage the whole platform, depending on your account.
